The purity threshold is the second most sensitive parameter. Configurations with a purity of 1.0 score 0.857 HOTA points below purity 0.6 on average. This confirms the distribution matching tradeoff described above: training exclusively on pure fragments means the model only sees clean feature vectors where the ReID embedding, jersey predictions, and team predictions consistently represent a single player. At inference time, however, many tracklets still contain identity switches that the splitter missed. These impure tracklets produce blended features that the model has not learned to handle, degrading its merge predictions. Lowering the threshold to 0.6 introduces some label noise but exposes the model to the same blended feature patterns it encounters during inference, resulting in better generalization.
